# Overview of the Project
BrightTV is a subscription-based streaming platform operating in South Africa. The CVM (Customer Value Management) team has commissioned this analysis to identify growth levers, understand viewer behaviour, and develop a data-driven strategy for expanding the subscriber base during the current financial year.

The analysis draws on 10,000 viewing events from 4,386 unique users and is structured around four core questions: who is watching, where they are watching from, when they watch, and what drives their consumption. Findings are translated into six strategic initiatives with a sequenced 90-day activation roadmap.

# Aim of the Project
The objective was to anlyse the existing data to develop ways to increase usage and consumption from viewership point of view.

- Drivers of Consumption

Five key factors that influence session length:

-- Daypart: Afternoon sessions are 45% longer than evening (10.7 vs 7.4 min).
-- Day of week: Saturdays drive sessions 50% longer than Mondays.
-- Age group:  Adults and Youth watch ~55% longer than Teens (9.4 vs 6.1 min).
-- Content type: Sport and live events sustain the longest watch times.
-- Geography: Gauteng users exhibit higher session frequency and multi-channel behaviour.

- Content Strategy for Low-Consumption Days

 -- Monday views are 41% below the Saturday peak. Recovering just 50% of this gap equates to approximately 340 extra sessions per week per 10,000 users.
